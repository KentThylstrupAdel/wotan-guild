Guild Rules

Wotan is a social guild with a focus on having fun, playing together and maintaining a good atmosphere.

1. Don't be a dick

Treat guild members and other players with respect. Banter is welcome; harassment and personal attacks are not.

2. Help each other

We're a guild. Group up, share knowledge and help your fellow members when you can.

3. Keep drama out of guild chat

Disagreements happen. Handle them like adults instead of turning guild chat or Discord into a battleground.

4. PvP happens

We play Horde on a PvP server. Expect world PvP, and don't be afraid to call for backup when the Alliance gets ideas above their station.

5. Real life comes first

Wotan is a game, not a job. There is no expectation that members prioritise World of Warcraft over real-life responsibilities.
